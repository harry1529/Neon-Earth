## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an uncompromising, high-velocity cyberpunk interface tailored for deep-space telemetry, planetary control matrices, and dystopian planetary networks. It merges tactile militaristic head-up displays (HUDs) with luminous atmospheric glassmorphism.

### Core Principles
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep void blacks juxtaposed against radiant photonic emissions create a multi-layered terminal environment.
- **Instrument Precision:** Surfaces convey dense tactical data, utilizing monospaced metrics, micro-grids, telemetry badges, and laser-precise focal points.
- **Vitreous Refraction:** Translucent panels with subtle chromatic dispersion simulate holographic glass projected in a low-light command cockpit.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Neon accents indicate critical status and interactive elements rather than arbitrary decoration, maintaining high scanability and ergonomic endurance in dark environments.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for pure dark-mode immersion, deploying high-contrast neon emitters over stratified obsidian foundations.

### Token Architecture
- **Primary (`#00f0ff` - Electric Cyan):** Primary tactical action token, HUD reticles, active telemetry traces, and confirmed link states.
- **Secondary (`#d946ef` - Ultraviolet Magenta):** Auxiliary alerts, secondary interactive paths, synthetic biological status, and sub-system overrides.
- **Tertiary (`#f59e0b` - Amber Laser):** Cautionary thresholds, hardware alerts, kinetic telemetry warnings, and elevated clearance locks.
- **Neutral Foundation (`#070b14` / `#030712` - Void / Obsidian):** Structural substrate, viewport canvas, and low-reflectance structural framing.
- **Sub-Layer Accents (`#0f172a` to `#1e1b4b` - Abyssal Navy):** Deep container surfaces, recessive table headers, and dormant HUD grids.

### Luminescence Guidelines
- Glow effects (`box-shadow` or `drop-shadow`) must not exceed an opacity of `0.35` for sustained readouts to prevent visual vibration.
- Interactive hover and focus triggers induce a dynamic flare of the primary or secondary emission token up to `0.6` opacity with zero ambient blur expansion beyond `12px`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy divides responsibilities strictly across three kinetic roles:
- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Angular, high-tension structural geometry for terminal names, sector alerts, and major telemetry headers. Always rendered with neutral-100 or tinted electric cyan accents.
- **Interface Body (`Inter`):** Neutral, hyper-legible humanist sans designed for high-density scanning, complex mission logs, and narrative dossiers.
- **Labels, Telemetry, and Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`):** Fixed-width monospaced precision for coordinate streams, sensor feeds, timestamps, and input prompts. Uppercase by default with `+0.08em` letter spacing for micro-telemetry tokens.

## Layout & Spacing

The viewport implements a structured 12-column fluid grid system across desktop viewports, condensing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile handsets.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Rhythms:** Modules anchor to a strict 8px base grid. All margins and gutters maintain rigid geometric alignment to emulate synthetic tactical consoles.
- **Viewport Canvas:** Side gutters maintain minimum safe margins, housing persistent coordinate indices, vertical edge rulers, and system breadcrumbs.
- **Responsive Adaptations:** Desktop arrangements prioritize multi-pane HUD arrangements (split telemetry monitors, control sidebars). Under 768px, peripheral monitors collapse into modular, tab-driven contextual overlays with persistent bottom-pinned command bars.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy operates through atmospheric backdrops, translucent refractive layering, and focused photonic glow.

### Depth Archetypes
- **Base Substrate (Level 0):** Pure void (`#030712`) layered with low-opacity vector gridlines (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.03)`).
- **HUD Glass Paneling (Level 1):** Translucent abyss container fill (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)`) paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a 1px border rendered in `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Active Deck / Floating Windows (Level 2):** Elevated glass (`rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.75)`) bounded by dual-tone borders: top/left highlighted at `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)` and bottom/right cast in `rgba(217, 70, 239, 0.2)`.
- **Target Reticles & Overlays (Level 3):** Modal components and critical overrides with radial back-glows (`0 0 30px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`), framing the focal space without obscuring underlying real-time spatial context.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a tight, engineered aesthetic:
- **Corner Radii:** Micro-radii (`roundedness: 1`, `0.25rem`) dominate primary interface panels, buttons, and badges, avoiding organic roundness in favor of industrial precision.
- **Chamfers & Angles:** Critical operational modules may employ a 45-degree corner clip (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) on top-right and bottom-left bounds to reinforce militaristic hardware housing.
- **Dividers:** Thin, segmented hairline vectors (0.5px to 1px) with dot-matrix joints demarcate modular container bounds.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Cyan Vector):** Solid electric cyan fill (`#00f0ff`) with deep obsidian text (`#030712`), uppercase `JetBrains Mono` label, and corner-bracket stroke lines. Hover states trigger a high-intensity cyan drop shadow (`0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.6)`).
- **Secondary Action (Ultraviolet Hollow):** Transparent dark slate base with a 1px border of vivid magenta (`#d946ef`) and text to match. Hover induces an interior glow sweep (`rgba(217, 70, 239, 0.15)`).
- **Destructive / Caution (Laser Amber):** Hairline amber border (`#f59e0b`) paired with flashing diagnostic corner tabs.

### Cards & Telemetry Pods
- Translucent frosted glass containers featuring micro-headers with numeric status prefixes (e.g., `SEC_04 // ONLINE`).
- Border construction features low-opacity base outlines with high-intensity accent marks on opposite diagonal corners.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact `JetBrains Mono` status monitors with pulse indicators. Green/cyan for nominal operational states, magenta for synthetic overrides, and laser amber for threshold limits.
- Background fills remain tinted at `0.1` opacity, framed by solid 1px tracking borders.

### Input Fields
- Underlined or recessed dark-well inputs (`rgba(3, 7, 18, 0.8)`) topped with uppercase micro-labels.
- Active focus state ignites a 1px electric cyan border and reveals an animated corner telemetry bracket.

### Telemetry Bars & Scanners
- Segmented data meters comprised of vertical dashes or continuous linear bars with high-luminance trail animations for live throughput tracking.
